Keypause Source on GitHub

Press one button.
Wipe your keyboard.
Done.

Keypause switches every key on your Mac off for a minute or two while you clean, then switches them back on. A stray ⌘W can't close your window, and a brush stroke can't send half a message.

brew install --cask destucr/tap/keypause
Download the DMG

Free, MIT licensed. macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon.

What it does

Turns every key off
Letters, shortcuts, function keys and the media keys. Your mouse and trackpad keep working, so Unlock is always one click away.
Starts from anywhere
Press ⌃⌥⌘K, click the keycap in the menu bar, or run keypause start 90 in Terminal for 90 seconds.
Stays out of your way
The small island at the top of the screen doesn't take focus, so a video keeps playing while you clean. The screen stays awake. On a notched MacBook the island grows out of the notch.
Always ends
You pick 30 seconds to 10 minutes. When the timer runs out the keys come back on their own. Instead of a progress bar, a night turns to sunrise and the keycap mascot wakes up.
Keeps to itself
It never reads, stores or sends what you type. The only network request is an optional update check, once a day at most, against this project's GitHub releases.

Good to know

  • It asks for Accessibility permission. That is how macOS lets an app intercept key presses. The welcome window walks you through it.
  • It isn't notarized, because that needs a paid Apple developer account. Homebrew skips the warning. If you download the DMG, right-click the app and choose Open the first time. 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Keypause.app
  • The power button still works. No software can block it.
